UAE ENVIRONMENT PROJECT

A cooler school can be a smarter school.

Smart Eco-School is a student-designed idea for reducing the heat entering school buildings and cutting unnecessary energy use — while keeping learning spaces comfortable.

Explore the idea

Our model: reflective roof + shade + ventilation + daylight + renewable energy.

The Problem

The UAE has a hot climate, so keeping indoor spaces comfortable can require substantial cooling. Every time a school reduces the amount of heat entering a classroom, it can potentially reduce the work required from air-conditioning systems.

☀️

Strong sunlight

Roofs, walls and windows can receive intense solar heat.

❄️

Cooling demand

Air conditioning helps maintain comfortable learning spaces.

⚡

Energy use

Cooling is one part of a school's overall electricity demand.

🌍

Why it matters

Using energy efficiently means less unnecessary energy is consumed.

Our Solution: Smart Eco-School

Our physical model demonstrates practical design choices that can help a school reduce heat gain and use energy more intelligently.

☀️ Reflective roof
Reflective surfaces can reduce solar heat absorbed by the roof.
🌳 Shade & greenery
Trees and shaded areas can block direct sunlight around learning spaces.
🪟 Shaded windows
External shading can reduce direct solar radiation entering rooms.
🌬️ Natural ventilation
Well-placed openings can support airflow when outdoor conditions allow.
💡 Daylight
Good window placement can make useful daylight available during the day.
☀️ Solar energy
Solar panels can generate renewable electricity for suitable school loads.

Take Action

Students don't control the whole building — but small actions can still reduce unnecessary energy use and build better habits.

Turn off lights
Switch off lights when a room is empty or daylight is enough.
Keep doors closed
When cooling is operating, avoid leaving cooled spaces open unnecessarily.
Use daylight
Make use of natural light when it provides enough visibility.
Report waste
Tell a teacher or facilities team about lights, taps or equipment being left on unnecessarily.
Protect green spaces
Care for trees and plants around the school.
Start an Eco-Team
Work with classmates to track simple environmental improvements.

The Physical Model

What judges can see

A miniature UAE school made primarily from ordinary/recycled materials. The model shows how roof reflection, shading, ventilation, daylight, greenery and renewable energy can work together.

The model is the physical proof of the idea. The website explains the problem, the actions and the potential impact.

Project message

“Don't only cool the school. Design it to stay cooler.”

A concept for smarter energy use in UAE schools.

S

Founder of the Smart Eco-School Movement

Shourya Swarnkar

Shourya Swarnkar is the student founder of the Smart Eco-School movement, a project created to show how UAE schools can become more environmentally responsible through simple, practical ideas.

The movement combines a physical school model with an interactive website. Its goal is to help students understand energy use, reduce unnecessary waste, and think about smarter school design.

Movement message: Small ideas can create a smarter, cleaner school.
